About this project

Created by lfarci for my girlfriend, who is learning C#. I built this tracker to encourage her to complete the book’s exercises and celebrate her progress, one Challenge at a time.

C# Quest Tracker is an unofficial companion to The C# Player’s Guide, Fifth Edition, by RB Whitaker. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by RB Whitaker.

Buy the PDF edition Buy the print edition on Amazon

Your progress is stored in this browser and is never sent to a server. Once the tracker has loaded, the current tab works offline; reopening or refreshing while offline isn’t guaranteed.
